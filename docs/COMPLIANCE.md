# Compliance

You operate this system. HavenID does not give legal advice.

Call recording and transcription can be illegal without consent depending on the US state and country. The dashboard keeps recording **off** until you enable voicemail **and** check the legal acknowledgement.

TCPA and similar rules apply to outbound calling and some autodial behavior. This MVP only answers inbound Twilio numbers and forwards to numbers you configure.

You are responsible for:

- Informing callers if you record
- Using a number and caller ID you are allowed to use
- Twilio acceptable use
- Data retention of contacts and recordings on your VPS
